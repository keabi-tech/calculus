FOUNDATION · 01

Introduction

Before studying differentiation and integration, we need to understand what mathematical functions represent.

Definition · Function

A function is a mathematical relationship that assigns exactly one output to each permitted input.

y = x²

This equation tells us that the output value, represented by y, is the square of the input value, represented by x.

Input xCalculationOutput y
11 × 11
22 × 24
33 × 39
44 × 416
Important idea

Differentiation studies how the output of a function changes as its input changes. Integration helps us recover accumulated quantities from their rates of change.

FOUNDATION · 02

Algebra foundations

Differentiation and integration use algebraic expressions. Understanding powers, coefficients, constants, and terms makes the rules easier.

1. Terms

5x³ + 4x² + 7x + 9

The expression contains four terms:

  • 5x³: coefficient 5, variable x, power 3.
  • 4x²: coefficient 4, variable x, power 2.
  • 7x: coefficient 7, variable x, power 1.
  • 9: constant term.

2. Powers

x³ = x × x × x

A power tells us how many times a variable is multiplied by itself.

3. Important algebra rules

Multiplication

x² × x³ = x⁵

Add the powers when multiplying the same base.

Division

x⁵ ÷ x² = x³

Subtract the powers when dividing the same base.

Power of a power

(x²)³ = x⁶

Multiply the powers.

Zero power

x⁰ = 1

For nonzero x, x⁰ equals one.

PART I · DIFFERENTIATION · 01

Meaning of a derivative

Definition

A derivative measures the instantaneous rate at which a function changes with respect to its input variable.

Consider a moving object. Its position changes as time passes. The derivative of its position describes its instantaneous velocity.

In geometry, a derivative represents the slope of a tangent line to a curve at a particular point.

Visual example: Slope of a curve

A tangent line touches the curve at the selected point. Its slope represents the derivative there.

Derivative notation

y = x²
dy/dx

The notation dy/dx means the derivative of y with respect to x.
